Ziqing Pan

PhD Candidate · Cornell University · Computational Biology

I am a PhD candidate working on computational biology. My work sits at the intersection of human statistical genetics, population genetics, machine learning and genomics, with a focus on developing efficient algorithms for analyzing large-scale biological data.

I am advised by Prof. April Wei and affiliated with the department of Computational Biology at Cornell University. Previously, I studied at Sun Yat-sen University, UC Berkeley, UCLA and Columbia University.
